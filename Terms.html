The System helps you record, calculate and report church offerings. It is a tool to assist you. It does not replace your church’s own financial controls, approvals, receipts or accounting.

2. Use it responsibly

  • Use the System only for lawful and honest purposes.
  • Enter information that is accurate and that you are allowed to record.

3. Protect your accounts, devices and data

Your records are stored in the browser on your device. You are responsible for keeping that device, its browser and your Google account (if you use Google Drive backup) secure and for deciding who can use them. Anyone who can open the System on your device, or sign in to your Google account, may be able to see or change the records.

4. Verify your records and backups

Please check your totals, reports and receipts yourself. If you use Google Drive backup, check from time to time that backups are being made and that you know how to restore them. Restoring a backup replaces the records on the device with the backup’s contents, so read the confirmation message carefully. Keeping your own extra copies of important records is recommended.

Google Drive backup is optional and works only after you give permission in Google’s own sign-in screen. Your use of Google services is also subject to Google’s terms. You can disconnect at any time using the Disconnect button on the Backup page. See the Privacy Policy for details.
