Privacy Policy

Church Offering System · Last updated: 19 October 2026

This page explains, in simple words, what information the Church Offering System (the “System”) handles and how the optional Google Drive backup works.

1. Information the System handles

Only what you type into it or choose in it: offering records (dates, offering types, amounts and totals), member names and receipt numbers you enter, custom offering names, calculator history, and your settings (for example your backup preferences and the name of your backup folder).

2. Data stored on your device

If you turn on Offering System Lock, the System saves on your device only an identifier and a public verification key for your device’s screen lock. It never sees or stores your fingerprint, PIN, pattern or password. The lock hides the app screen until you verify; it does not encrypt your records.

Your records are stored in your browser on your own device, so the System can work without internet. This data stays with that browser and device. Clearing the browser’s site data, or removing the browser or device, can delete it. The System does not have its own online database that receives your records.

3. Optional Google Drive backup

If you choose to use it, you connect your Google account and Google asks for your permission. The System requests only access to files and folders that it creates or that you open with it (the “drive.file” permission); it is not given access to the rest of your Drive.

  • After you save a folder name, the System uses that one folder in your Google Drive and keeps one backup file in it. The file contains a copy of your records and settings.
  • Backup and restore happen directly between your device and Google Drive. Restoring replaces the records on your device with the backup after you confirm.
  • The System reads your Google account email address only to show which account is connected and to keep each account’s backup folder separate. It is remembered on your device.
  • If you connect the same Google account on another device, the System looks for the backup folder that account already uses.

4. Google authorization and how to disconnect

Google Drive access works only while you have given authorization. Google gives the System a short-lived access code (valid for about an hour). It is kept in your browser on your device, and it is removed when you disconnect or when it expires or stops working. You may be asked to authorize again.

To disconnect: open the Backup page in the System and tap Disconnect. Disconnecting stops further Google Drive requests. It does not delete the records on your device or the backup already in your Google Drive; you can delete the folder or file in Google Drive yourself. You can also review or remove the System’s access in your Google Account settings under security / third-party access.

5. Protect your Google account and your devices

Anyone who can use your unlocked device or browser, or sign in to your Google account, may be able to see your records and backup. Please use a screen lock and protect your Google account. The System does not add its own password or encryption to the data stored on your device or to the backup file, so its protection depends on your device and your Google account.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your information. The System does not send your offering records to us. They go only to the places you choose, such as your own Google Drive. Some buttons open other websites (for example the video guide or WhatsApp); those sites have their own privacy practices. The company that hosts this website may keep ordinary web server records (such as visits to the page); those records are outside the System’s control and do not contain your offering records.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.

8. Questions

For questions about privacy, please use the Contact Us section inside the System, or email info.ekambani@gmail.com.
